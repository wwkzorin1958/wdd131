Planning your hike

The MacLehose Trail is well-marked and can be done in sections or as a thru-hike. Best months are October to April. Always carry enough water — some sections have no reliable supplies. Public transport connects most section start/end points.

  • 🚌 Buses & minibuses to trailheads
  • 💧 Carry 2–3 L water per person
  • ☀️ Sun protection essential
  • 📱 Mobile coverage varies
