🏔️ MacLehose Trail

Plan Your Hike

Everything you need to know before setting out on the MacLehose Trail — transport, supplies, weather, and safety.

Best time to hike

October to April offers the most comfortable conditions — cooler temperatures, lower humidity, and clearer skies. Avoid July–September (typhoon season and extreme heat).

Getting to the trail

Public transport connects most section start and end points. Buses and minibuses run regularly to trailheads. Taxis are available but can be expensive for remote sections.

What to bring

  • 💧 2–3 L water per person
  • ☀️ Sun protection (hat, sunscreen)
  • 🥾 Sturdy hiking shoes
  • 🍫 High-energy snacks
  • 📱 Fully charged phone + power bank
  • 🧴 Insect repellent

Safety notes

Mobile coverage varies — some sections have no signal. Always tell someone your plans. Start early to avoid hiking in darkness. Check weather forecasts before setting out.
